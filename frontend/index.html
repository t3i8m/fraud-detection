Model

An XGBoost classifier, tuned for one honest test

Why two models, not one

Online and in-person spending leave very different footprints, so each channel gets its own tuned model instead of forcing one classifier to learn both behaviors at once.

Methodology

Two decisions that keep this model honest

Both exist to stop the future from quietly leaking into the past during training.

Channel isolation

Online and in-person purchase histories are kept completely separate when building a customer's behavioral profile. A customer's card-present shopping habits say nothing reliable about their online habits, and mixing them blurs the signal the model actually needs.

14-day trust quarantine

A transaction only counts toward a customer's trusted history once 14 days have passed without a confirmed fraud report, mirroring how long real fraud confirmation actually takes.

Why this matters
Naive approach
  • Mixes online and in-person history into one profile
  • Trusts a transaction the instant it happens
  • One model tries to learn every channel at once
This system
  • Builds a separate behavioral profile per channel
  • Waits 14 days before a transaction is trusted
  • A dedicated, separately tuned model per channel
